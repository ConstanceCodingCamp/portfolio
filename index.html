Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do you help with?

I help founders turn unclear ideas into structured, buildable systems. That includes clarifying what to build,
how it should function, and what should be prioritized so execution is focused and efficient.

Do you build websites or apps?

No. My focus is not production development. I focus on product strategy, system design, and execution planning so you
or your team can build with clarity and direction.

How is this different from hiring a developer?

Developers build what’s already defined. I help define what should be built, why it matters, and how it fits into your overall
system so execution isn’t based on guesswork.

How do we get started?

You start with a clarity session where we break down your idea, identify priorities, and map the structure needed to move forward.
