Superior Technology Solutions · Documented internal case study · October 2026

Incident Identification & Risk Assessment

Part of a single documented investigation of the susienewton.com HTTPS certificate incident.

Situation

A visitor-facing Chrome warning identified a certificate hostname mismatch on susienewton.com (NET::ERR_CERT_COMMON_NAME_INVALID).

Task

Document the issue, assess its impact on visitors, and isolate the responsible hosting and certificate settings.

Action

Reviewed browser warning evidence, domain records, and GitHub Pages custom-domain status.

Result & evidence

A documented incident and focused investigation plan. The exact start time and duration of the fault were not established.

This is an STS-owned website incident, not a third-party client case. Outcomes are limited to the evidence observed during troubleshooting.
